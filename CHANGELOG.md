# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (Minecraft 26.3, Fabric)

First release.

- Tracker book on **J** (changeable in Controls) with five tabs: Overview, Mobs, Items & Blocks, Advancements, Chronicle.
- Mobs: every mob counts once, 15 mobs also count their variants (sheep colours, cat breeds, wolf types, cow/pig/chicken climates, horse colours, axolotls, pandas, villager types and more).
- Items & Blocks: all 1,536 items you can get in survival. Colour variants count separately, enchantments do not.
- Advancements: all 126, with sub-lists for multi-part ones (biomes, foods, mobs, breeding, cats, wolves).
- Hints and four difficulty levels for everything; filter by category, difficulty and status, sort, and search.
- Chronicle: an advancement-style timeline by Minecraft day, ten entries per day with the rest on hover.
- Existing worlds: on first start the mod reads statistics, advancements, inventory and ender chest, and estimates the day for earlier entries. Mob variants killed before installation cannot be recovered.
- Works in single player and on servers (the mod must be installed on the server and the client).
- English and German.
