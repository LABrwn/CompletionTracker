# Completion Tracker

**Your road to 100 %: every mob killed, every item collected, every advancement reached.**

Press **J** to open a book that tracks your whole world:

- **Overview**: your overall progress, progress per area by difficulty, the easiest things still open, and what you reached most recently.
- **Mobs**: every mob in the game. 15 mobs also count their variants: sheep colours, cat breeds, wolf types, cow, pig and chicken climates, horse colours, rabbits, parrots, frogs, foxes, llamas, mooshrooms, axolotls (yes, the blue one), pandas and villager types.
- **Items & Blocks**: all 1,536 items you can get in survival. Colour variants count separately, enchantments do not.
- **Advancements**: all 126, with a checklist for multi-part ones. See which biomes, foods, mobs or cat breeds are still missing.
- **Chronicle**: a timeline in the style of the advancement screen, by Minecraft day. Ten entries per day; the rest shows when you hover over the "…" frame.

Every entry has a **hint** on how to get it and one of four **difficulty levels**. Filter by category, difficulty and status, sort by name or difficulty, and search.

## Works with existing worlds

On first start the mod reads what Minecraft already knows: statistics, advancements, inventory and ender chest. Entries from before installation get an estimated day. Minecraft does not store which variant of a mob you killed, so variants count from installation on.

## Details

- Fabric, requires **Fabric API**.
- Single player and servers. On a server, install the mod on the server **and** on every client.
- Follows your game language. English and German included.
- Stores its data in `<world>/completiontracker/`. Your world files are never changed.

# Sammel-Tracker (Deutsch)

**Dein Weg zu 100 %: jeder Mob getötet, jedes Item gesammelt, jeder Fortschritt erreicht.**

Mit **J** öffnest du ein Buch mit Übersicht, Mobs (inklusive Varianten), Items & Blöcken, Fortschritten und einer Chronik nach Minecraft-Tagen. Zu jedem Eintrag gibt es einen Hinweis und eine Schwierigkeitsstufe. Bestehende Welten werden beim ersten Start eingelesen. Benötigt Fabric API; auf Servern muss die Mod auf Server und Client installiert sein.
